Archetype List

Crazy

Crazies are the nut jobs who love what happened. Driven mad by... something, Crazies just go, and go, and go, whipping around from one thing to the next, usually in a whirlwind of violence and blood.

Crazies look like... anything. They tend to pierce themselves, tattoo up, and engage in all manner of dress that stands out. You’ll know a Crazy when you see one.

Crazy Attributes

  • 6 Hit Points
  • Crazies start with the Insane Archetype trait:
    • You’re insane. Whenever you would take a risk that would make anyone else pause, you gain Advantage on the Test.

Fixer

Fixers are the ones trying to put the pieces back together. They take the stuff the Scavengers bring in and try to use it to better the lives of those around them... or gain power.

Fixers look like Normals but usually covered a bit more in grease and iron filings.

Fixer Attributes

  • 6 Hit Points
  • Fixers start with the Mechanic Archetype trait.
    • You can test with Advantage when attempting to fix mechanical devices or jerry-rig a simple tool

<div class="missing-image">Image not included in the supplied source files: <strong>FixerIMG.png</strong></div>

Mutant

Warped by whatever caused the apocalypse, Mutants are distorted versions of humankind and tend to live in their own little enclaves, as they’re not frequently welcome everywhere. Mutantkind ranges all over the board, from brutes to skinny four-armed weirdos who worship cactuses.

Mutants almost always look like humans taken to the extreme. They’re either too big and muscle bound, too skinny and pale, or anything in between. It’s always just weird to see a mutie.

Mutant Attributes

  • 8 Hit Points
  • Mutants start with the Archetype trait Mutation:
    • You can select up to 3 Mutation Traits (instead of one).

<div class="missing-image">Image not included in the supplied source files: <strong>MutantIMG.png</strong></div>

Normals

Normals are your run of the mill, average humans. They tend to be farmers, scavengers, or living in settlements, trying to repair civilization and get on with their lives as best they can. Normals are humans, so anything today is certainly there. And probably a little weirder.

Normals Attributes

  • 6 Hit Points
  • Humans select an additional Trait from the Wasteland Traits List.

Scavenger

Scavengers are those who wander the waste with the express intent of finding lost bits of stuff and putting it to use. Scavengers are hardy folk who are used to the harsh life in the wastes, but they’re usually welcome in settlements due to the supplies they often have.

Scavengers tend to not stand out too much and wearclothing that allows them to quickly hide or worm their way through ruins, relics, and pointy warrens and tunnels.

Scavenger Attributes

  • 7 Hit Points
  • Scavengers start with the Digger Archetype:
    • You gain Advantage on Scavenge Tests and can roll twice when rolling to find Scavenge

<div class="missing-image">Image not included in the supplied source files: <strong>ScavengerIMG.png</strong></div>

Survivor

You survive. That’s it. You’re the one who gets back up every time you’re knocked down. You don’t know when to quit, and you just keep going.

Survivors tend to look like Normals, but with a lot more scars and a grim haunted look in their eyes, due to all the horrors they’ve seen out there in the wastes.

Survivor Attributes

  • 6 Hit Points
  • Survivors start with the Archetype trait Always Prepared:
    • You know how to survive. You gain Advantage on Tests to find shelter or aid (not supplies or scavenging though).

<div class="missing-image">Image not included in the supplied source files: <strong>SurvivorIMG.png</strong></div>

Tyrants

Tyrants are the ones who take over settlements and enclaves, forcing their will on the people. Warlords and generals, the Tyrants are stuck in perpetual war: against the world, against others, and against themselves.

Tyrants tend to dress better, tougher, or fancier than everyone else. Status has rewards, and you have to look the part.

Tyrant Attributes

  • 6 Hit Points
  • Tyrants start with the Commanding Archetype trait:
    • When you’re attacked, you can impose your will on your foe. Roll 1d6. If successful the attack misses. You cannot use this ability if you Evade.

<div class="missing-image">Image not included in the supplied source files: <strong>TyrantIMG.png</strong></div>

Wanderer

Wanderers are those who move from place to place throughout the wastelands, just picking up the pieces as they go. They don’t get attached, they don’t get nothing. Just moving from one place to another.

Wanderers tend to look a lot like Scavengers, but even more ragged.

Wanderer Attributes

  • 6 Hit Points
  • Wanderers start with the Archetype trait Unattached:
    • Anytime anyone tries to persuade you, intimidate you, or threaten you, they have Disadvantage.
