Player Progression

At the end of every session, the GM will award experience.

  • Achieve 'A' goal - 3 pt
  • Achieve 'B' goal - 2 pt
  • Achieve 'C' goal - 1 pt

You may buy upgrades with the following costs.

  • 5 Experience = a permanent increase to your HP of 1.
  • 7 Experience = a new Proficient or Mastered Weapon.
  • 9 Experience = a new Trait.
