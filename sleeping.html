Sleeping

You need at least 6 in-game hours of uninterrupted sleep and 1 ration every day to regain your strength. If you manage to obtain 6 hours of uninterrupted sleep and consume 1 ration, you will fully restore any lost Hit Points to your maximum. If you cannot get a full 6 hours of sleep, you can regain 1 Hit Point for every hour of sleep. When asleep, Survivors are unconscious. If something is going on around them, they are less likely to be aware of it, and have Disadvantage when making a Test to see if something wakes them up.

You will need to eat 1 ration and get 6 hours of sleep everyday to avoid the possibility of Conditions

  • 1st day without sleep and ration - test with disadvantage to see if you are exhausted
  • 2nd day without sleep and ration - automatic fail
