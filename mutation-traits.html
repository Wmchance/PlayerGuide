Mutation Traits

Warped by whatever ravaged the world, something is odd or unusual about you at a genetic level.

A character can only have 1 Mutation Trait.

Adaptive Reflexes

“You move before your brain catches up.”

When you fail an Evade Test, you may re-Test.

Genetic Memory

“Got a memory screaming at me…”

When Testing to see if you know something, if you fail, you can re-Test with Disadvantage.

Environmental Camo

“Surprise, meatbag!”

All Tests to locate you when you are hidden are at Disadvantage.

Bulging Muscles

“You look like you could punch through concrete.”

When you are Testing to lift, carry, or budge something, you gain Advantage.

Your melee attacks do +1 damage.

Third Eye

“If you’re lucky, it’s in your forehead.”

When you fail a Perception-related Test, you can choose to re-Test it at Disadvantage.

Jumpin’ Jack

“You get around a lot better than you should.”

You gain Advantage on any Test related to jumping, running, or moving around.

Bone Spines

“Never disarmed.”

You can protrude Bone Spines as a melee or ranged weapon from your body. It takes an action to deploy it.

You gain Advantage on the first attack each combat with this weapon.

Counts as both Light Melee and Ranged weapons.

Scales and Stuff

“Hard to kill.”

You gain +2 Hit Points.

If you have the Diehard Trait, you can use it one additional time per day.
