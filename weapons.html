Weapons

Light Melee:

  • Can be used one-handed
  • Can be stowed & drawn as a free action
  • Has a range of 5'

Heavy Melee:

  • Must be used two-handed
  • Can be held one-handed but take an action to stow & draw
    • actions taken with one hand are done with disadvantage (e.g. pulling a rope or freeing something from rubble)
  • Has a range of 10'

Light Ranged:

  • Can be used one-handed
  • Can be stowed & drawn as a free action
  • Have a range of 30'
  • Can't be used within 5'
  • Can be fired and reloaded with 1AP

Heavy Ranged:

  • Must be used two-handed
  • Can be held one-handed but take an action to stow & draw
    • actions taken with one hand are done with disadvantage (e.g. pulling a rope or freeing something from rubble)
  • Has a range of 60'
  • Can't be used within 10'
  • Takes 1AP to load and 1AP to fire
